# The Tribunals Reforms Act, 2026, section 9
(1) The Commission shall observe such procedure in regard to the holding and transaction of business at its meetings in such manner as may be specified by regulations.	(2) The Chairperson of the Commission shall preside over the meetings of the Commission.	(3) When the Chairperson of the Commission is unable to attend a meeting of the Commission for any reason, the senior-most Judicial Member of the Commission present at the meeting shall preside over it.	(4) All matters which are brought before the Commission, shall be decided by a majority of the Members of the Commission, including the Chairperson of the Commission, present and voting.	(5) The Chairperson of the Commission shall have a casting vote.
