# The Cattle-Trespass Act, 1871, section 27A
1[27A. Adjudication of penalties.—For the purpose of adjudicating penalties under the provisions of this Act and the rules made thereunder, the State Government or the Union territory administration, may authorise the Sub-Divisional Magistrate or any other Executive Magistrate, as the case may be, having jurisdiction, as an adjudicating officer for holding an inquiry and imposing penalties in such manner as may be prescribed by the Central Government:Provided that no such penalty shall be imposed without giving the person concerned a reasonable opportunity of being heard.]
