# The National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development Act, 1981, section 30A
  [30A. Bills rediscounting.--The National Bank may rediscount bills of exchange and promissory notes made, drawn, accepted or endorsed by any company or body corporate concerned with agriculture and rural development presented by a scheduled bank, a State co-operative bank, State land development bank, regional rural bank or any other institution or class of institutions approved by the Board.]
