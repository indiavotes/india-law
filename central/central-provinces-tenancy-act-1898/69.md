# The Central Provinces Tenancy Act, 1898, section 69
  Notwithstanding any contract to the contrary or any provision of a record-of-rights, an ordinary tenant shall not be ejected from his holding by his landlord as such except--
(a) as provided in the case of an occupancy tenant by section 52 ;
(b) in accordance with the provisions of section 66 ;
(c) in execution of a decree for ejectement passed on the ground that his holding consists entirely of sir-land.
