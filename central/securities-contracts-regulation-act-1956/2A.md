# The Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956, section 2A
  1[2A. Interpretation of certain words and expressions.--Words and expressions used herein and not defined in this Act but defined in the Companies Act, 1956 (1 of 1956) or the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992 (15 of 1992) or the Depositories Act, 1996 (22 of 1996) shall have the same meanings respectively assigned to them in those Acts.]
