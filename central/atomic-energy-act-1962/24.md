# The Atomic Energy Act, 1962, section 24
  (1) Whoever--
(a) contravenes any order made under section 14 or any condition subject to which a licence is granted under that section; or
(b) contravenes any rule made under section 17 or any requirement, prohibition or restriction imposed under any such rule; or
(c) obstructs any person authorised by the Central Government under sub-section (4) of section 17 in the exercise of powers under that sub-section; or
(d) contravenes sub-section (2) of section 18;
shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to five years, or with fine, or with both.
