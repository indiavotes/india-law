# The Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu (Merger of Union territories) Act, 2019, section 10
  The total of the cash balances in all treasuries, the Reserve Bank of India, the State Bank of India and any nationalised bank, of the existing Union territories immediately before the appointed day shall be merged in the Union territory of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.
