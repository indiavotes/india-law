# The Companies (Profits) Surtax Act, 1964, section 23
  (1) A person shall not be proceeded against for an offence under section 20 or section 21 or section 22 or under the Indian Penal Code (45 of 1860) except at the instance of the 1 [Chief Commissioner or Commissioner.]
(2) The 1 [Chief Commissioner or Commissioner] may, either before or after the institution of proceedings, compound any offence punishable under section 20 or section 21 or section 22.
