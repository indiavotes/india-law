# The Prohibition of Benami Property Transactions Act, 1988, section 34
  1[34. Term of office of Chairperson and Members.--The Chairperson and Members of the Appellate Tribunal shall hold office for a term not exceeding five years from the date on which they enter upon their office, or until they attain the age of sixty-five years, whichever is earlier and shall not be eligible for reappointment.]
