# The General Clauses Act, 1897, section 6A
  1[6A. Repeal of Act making textual amendment in Act or Regulation.--Where any 2[Central Act] or Regulation made after the commencement of this Act repeals any enactment by which the text of any2[Central Act] or Regulation was amended by the express omission, insertion or substitution of any matter, then, unless a different intention appears, the repeal shall not affect the continuance of any such amendment made by the enactment so repealed and in operation at the time of such repeal.]
