# The Destructive Insects and Pests Act, 1914, section 4A
  1[4A. Power of Central Government to regulate or prohibit transport from State to State of insects or articles likely to infect.--The Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, prohibit or regulate, subject to such conditions as the Central Government may impose, the export from a State or the transport from one State to another State 2*** of any article or class of articles likely to cause infection to any crop or of insects generally or any class of insects.]
