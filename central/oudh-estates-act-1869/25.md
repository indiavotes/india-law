# The Oudh Estates' Act, 1869, section 25
  In the case of the grandparents, parents, and senior widows of the deceased, the maximum amount of the annuity shall be as follows :---
(a.) where the annual revenue payable to Government in respect of the estate is or exceeds 1,50,000 rupees⸻⸻a sum not exceeding 6,000 rupees :
(b.) where such revenue is or exceeds 100,000 rupees, but is less than 1,50,000 rupees a sum not exceeding 2,400 rupees :
(c.) where such revenue is or exceeds 50,000 rupees, but is less than 100,000 rupees ⸻⸻ a sum not exceeding 1,200 rupees :
(d.) where such revenue is or exceeds 25,000 rupees, but is less than 50,000 rupees ⸻⸻ a sum not exceeding 600 rupees :
(e.) where such revenue is or exceeds 15,000 rupees, but is less than 25,000 rupees ⸻⸻ a sum not exceeding 360 rupees :
(f.) where such revenue is or exceeds 7,000 rupees, but is less than 15,000 rupees ⸻⸻ a sum not exceeding 240 rupees ; and
(g.) where such revenue is less than 7,000 rupees⸻ a sum not exceeding 180 rupees.
Junior widows.— In the case of a junior widow of the deceased, the maximum amount of the annuity shall be one-half of the maximum amount to which a senior widow of the deceased would be entitled under the former part of this section.
