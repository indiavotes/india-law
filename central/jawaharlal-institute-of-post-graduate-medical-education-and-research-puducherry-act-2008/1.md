# The Jawaharlal Institute of Post-Graduate Medical Education and Research, Puducherry, Act, 2008, section 1
  (1) This Act may be called the Jawaharlal Institute of Post-Graduate Medical Education and Research, Puducherry Act, 2008.
(2) It shall come into force on such date 1 as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.
