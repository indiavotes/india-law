# The Railway Protection Force Act, 1957, section 3
  (1) There shall be constituted and maintained by the Central Government a 1[an armed Force of the Union] to be called the Railway Protection Force for the better protection and security of railway property.
(2) The force shall be constituted in such manner shall consist of such number of 2[superior officers subordinate officers, under officers and other enrolled members] of the Force and shall receive such pay and other renumeration as maybe prescribed.
