# The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016., section 67A
  1[67A. Fraudulent management of corporate debtor during pre-packaged insolvency resolution process.--On and after the pre-packaged insolvency commencement date, where an officer of the corporate debtor manages its affairs with the intent to defraud creditors of the corporate debtor or for any fraudulent purpose, the Adjudicating Authority may, on an application by the resolution professional, pass an order imposing upon any such officer, a penalty which shall not be less than one lakh rupees, but may extend to one crore rupees.]
