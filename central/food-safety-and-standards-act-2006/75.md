# The Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, section 75
Where, after taking cognizance of any offence, a Special Court is of the opinion that the offence is not triable by it, it shall, notwithstanding that it has no jurisdiction to try such offence, transfer the case for the trial of such offence to any court having jurisdiction under the 1[Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (46 of 2023)] and the court to which the case is transferred may proceed with the trial of the offence as if it had taken cognizance of the offence.
