# The Control of National Highways (Land and Traffic) Act, 2002, section 19
  Every appeal to the 1[Court] under this Act shall be preferred within a period of sixty days from the date on which the order appealed against has been made:
Provided that an appeal may be admitted after the expiry of the said period of sixty days, if the appellant satisfies the 1[Court] that he had sufficient cause for not preferring the appeal within the specified period.
