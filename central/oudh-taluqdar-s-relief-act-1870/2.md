# The Oudh Taluqdar's Relief Act, 1870, section 2
  In this Act---
"Chief Commissioner" means the Chief Commissioner of Oudh:
"aluqdr" means a person whose name is entered in the first of the lists mentioned in the Oudh "Estates" Act, 1869, section eight:
"heir" means the person for the time being entitled under the same Act as heir to a taluqdar.
