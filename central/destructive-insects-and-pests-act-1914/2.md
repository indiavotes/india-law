# The Destructive Insects and Pests Act, 1914, section 2
  In this Act, unless there is anything repugnant in the subject or context,--
(a) "crops" includes all agricultural or horticultural crops 1[and all trees, bushes or plants];
(b) "import" means the bringing or taking by sea, 2[land or air] 3[across any customs frontier as defined by the Central Government]; 4***
(c) "infection" means infection by any insect, fungus or other pest injurious to a crop; 5***
