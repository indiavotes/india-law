# The Merchant Shipping Act, 2025, section 175
  This Chapter shall apply to,---
(a) pollution damage caused by,---
(i) Indian vessel within the jurisdiction of any contracting party to the Civil Liability Convention; and
(ii) Indian vessel and any ship while it is at a port or place in India including the coastal waters, extending not more than two hundred nautical miles from the baseline;
(b) preventive measures, wherever taken, to prevent or minimise such pollution damage.
