# The Maritime Zones of India (Regulation of Fishing by Foreign Vessels) Act, 1981, section 13
  (1) Where any person is convicted of an offence under section 10 1***, the foreign vessel used in or in connection with the commission of the said offence, together with its fishing gear, equipment, stores and cargo and any fish on board such ship or the proceeds of the sale of any fish ordered to be sold under the second proviso to clause (a) of sub-section (4) of section 9 shall also be liable to confiscation.
(2) The foreign vessel or other things confiscated under sub-section (1) shall vest in the Central Government.
