# The Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 1957, section 116H
  1[116H. One-time taxic.--Notwithstanding anything contained in this Act, in those cases where the owner has already paid one-time tax under some scheme in the past, the extent of the rate able value on the basis of which the one-time tax was paid shall be set off against the annual value calculated under the provisions of the Delhi Municipal Corporation (Amendment) Act, 2003 and the tax liability shall be worked out on the basis of net annual value.
