# The Prisons Act, 1894, section 17
  Upon the death of a prisoner, the Jailer shall give immediate notice thereof to the Superintendent and the Medical Subordinate.
STATE AMENDMENT
Maharashtra.--
Amendment of section 17 of Act IX of 1894.--In section 17 of the principal Act, for the words "Medical Subordinate" the words "Medical Officer" shall be substituted.
[Vide Bombay Act XLV of 1959, s. 6]
