# The Weapons of Mass Destruction and their Delivery Systems (Prohibition of Unlawful Activities) Act, 2005, section 1
  (1) This Act may be called the Weapons of Mass Destruction and their Delivery Systems (Prohibition of Unlawful Activities) Act, 2005.
(2) It shall come into force on such date 1 as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.
