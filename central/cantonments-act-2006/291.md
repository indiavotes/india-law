# The Cantonments Act, 2006, section 291
Whoever in driving, leading or propelling a vehicle along a street fails, except in a case of actual necessity,---		(a) to keep to the left when passing a vehicle coming from the opposite direction; or		(b) to keep to the right when passing a vehicle going in the same direction as himself, shall be 1[liable to penalty] which may extend to five hundred rupees.
