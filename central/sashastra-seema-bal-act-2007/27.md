# The Sashastra Seema Bal Act, 2007, section 27
  Any person subject to this Act who commits any of the following offences, namely:–
(a) is guilty of any disgraceful conduct of a cruel, indecent or unnatural kind; or
(b) maligns, or feigns, or produces disease or infirmity in himself or intentionally delays his cure or aggravates his disease or infirmity; or
(c) with intent to render himself or any other person unfit for service, voluntarily causes hurt to himself or that person,
shall, on conviction by a Force Court, be liable to suffer imprisonment for a term which may extend to seven years or such less punishment as is in this Act mentioned.
