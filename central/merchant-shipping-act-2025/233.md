# The Merchant Shipping Act, 2025, section 233
  This Chapter shall apply to the wrecks located within the territory of India including the coastal waters but shall not apply to,---
(a) any measures taken under the International Convention relating to the Intervention on the High Seas in Cases of Oil Pollution Casualties, 1969;
(b) any warship or other vessel owned or operated by the Government for non-commercial service.
