# The Copyright Act, 1957, section 50A
  1[50A. Entries in the Register of Copyrights, etc., to be published.-- Every entry made in the Register of Copyrights or the particulars of any work entered under section 45, the correction of every entry made in such register under section 49, and every rectification ordered under section 50, shall be published by the Registrar of Copyrights in the Official Gazette or in such other manner as he may deem fit.]
