# The Merchant Shipping Act, 2025, section 203
  (1) The right to claim compensation under this Chapter shall be extinguished, if such claim is not made within a period of---
(a) three years from the date of occurrence of damage; and
(b) six years from the date of incident which caused such damage.
(2) Where the incident consists of a series of occurrences, the period of six years shall commence from the date of the first of such occurrence.
