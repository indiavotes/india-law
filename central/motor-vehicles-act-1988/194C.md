# The Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, section 194C
1[194C. Penalty for violation of safety measures for motor cycle drivers and pillion riders.--- Whoever drives a motor cycle or causes or allows a motor cycle to be driven in contravention of the provisions of section 128 or the rules or regulations made thereunder shall be 2[liable for a penalty]  of one thousand rupees and he shall be disqualified for holding licence for a period of three months.]
