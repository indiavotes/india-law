# The Chit Funds Act, 1982, section 85
  Nothing in this Act shall apply in respect of
(a) any chit started before the commencement of this Act; or
(b) any chit the amount of which, or where two or more chits were started or conducted simultaneously by the same foreman, the aggregate amount of which does not exceed 1[such amount as may be specified, by notification in the Official Gazette, by the State Goverment].
