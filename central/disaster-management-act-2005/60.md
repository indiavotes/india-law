# The Disaster Management Act, 2005, section 60
  No court shall take cognizance of an offence under this Act except on a complaint made by---
(a) the National Authority, the State Authority, the Central Government, the State Government, the District Authority 1[, the Urban Authority] or any other authority or officer authorised in this behalf by that Authority or Government, as the case may be; or
(b) any person who has given notice of not less than thirty days in the manner prescribed, of the alleged offence and his intention to make a complaint to the National Authority, the State Authority, the Central Government, the State Government, the District Authority 1[, the Urban Authority] or any other authority or officer authorised as aforesaid.
