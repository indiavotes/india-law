# The Multi-State Co-operative Societies Act, 2002, section 53
  (1) The board may, subject to such conditions as may be prescribed, constitute an Executive Committee and other committees or subcommittees as may be considered necessary:
Provided that other committees or sub-committees, other than the Executive Committee shall not exceed three.
(2) The Executive Committee or other committee or sub-committee referred to in sub-section (1) shall perform such functions as are assigned to it in accordance with the bye-laws of the multi-State cooperative society.
