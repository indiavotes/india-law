# The Rajiv Gandhi University Act, 2006, section 49
  In the North-Eastern Hill University Act, 1973,--
(i) in section 1, in sub-section (2), for the words "States of Meghalaya and Arunachal Pradesh", the words "State of Meghalaya" shall be substituted;
(ii) in section 2, in clause (l), for the words "States of Meghalaya and Arunachal Pradesh", the words "State of Meghalaya" shall be substituted;
(iii) in section 6, in sub-section (1), for the words "States of Meghalaya and Arunachal Pradesh", the words "State of Meghalaya" shall be substituted.
