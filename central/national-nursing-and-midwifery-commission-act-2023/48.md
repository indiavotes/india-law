# The National Nursing and Midwifery Commission Act, 2023, section 48
  No suit, prosecution or other legal proceeding shall lie against the Government, the National Commission or any Autonomous Board or a State Commission or any committee thereof, or any officer or other employee of the Government or of the National Commission acting under this Act for anything which is in good faith done or intended to be done under this Act or the rules or regulations made thereunder.
