# The Regional Centre for Biotechnology Act, 2016., section 22
  The following shall be the officers of the Regional Centre, namely:—
(i) Executive Director;
(ii) Deans;
(iii) Sub-Deans;
(iv) Associate Director (Administration);
(v) Registrar;
(vi) Finance Officer; and
(vii) such other officers as may be declared by the Statutes to be the officers of the Regional Centre.
