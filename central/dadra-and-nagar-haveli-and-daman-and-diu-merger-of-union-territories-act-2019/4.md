# The Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu (Merger of Union territories) Act, 2019, section 4
  In article 240 of the Constitution, in clause (1),--
(i) for entry (c), the following entry shall be substituted, namely:--
“(c) Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu;”;
(ii) entry (d) shall be omitted.
