# The North-Eastern Council Act, 1971, section 2
  In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,--
(a) "Council" means the North-Eastern Council set up under section 3;
1[(b) "north-eastern area" means the area comprising the States of Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram,2[Nagaland, Sikkim and Tripura]];
