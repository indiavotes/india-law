# The Forward Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1952, section 14B
  1[14B. Grant or refusal of certificate of registration.--On receipt of an application under section 14A, the Commission, after making such inquiry as it considers necessary in this behalf, may by order in writing grant a certificate of registration or refuse to grant it:
Provided that before refusing to grant such certificate, the association shall be given an opportunity of beng heard in the matter.]
