# The Rajghat Samadhi Act, 1951, section 5
  Subject to such rules as may be made under this Act, the powers and duties of the Committee shall be--
(a) to administer the affairs of the Samadhi and to keep the Samadhi in proper order and in a state of good repair;
(b) to organise and regulate periodical functions at the Samadhi;
(c) to do such other things as may be incidental or conducive to the efficient administration of the affairs of the Samadhi.
