# The Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019, section 23
  (1) The Lieutenant Governor may address the Legislative Assembly and may for that purpose require the attendance of members.
(2) The Lieutenant Governor may also send messages to the Legislative Assembly whether with respect to a Bill then pending in the Legislative Assembly or otherwise, and when a message so sent, the Legislative Assembly shall with all convenient despatch consider any matter required by the message to be taken into consideration.
