# The Navy Act, 1957, section 51
  Every person subject to naval law who without being guilty of desertion improperly leaves his ship or place of duty 1[or any place where he is required to be] or is absent without leave shall be punished with imprisonment for a term which may extend to two years or such other punishment as is hereinafter mentioned and shall also be punished by such mulcts of pay and allowances as may be prescribed.
