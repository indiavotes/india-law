# The Cantonments Act, 2006, section 145
(1) If any building or land, whether tenantable or otherwise, is---		(i) in an insanitary, filthy or unwholesome state; or		(ii) in the opinion of the Chief Executive Officer, a nuisance to persons residing in the neighbourhood; or		(iii) overgrown with prickly-pear or rank and noisome vegetation the Chief Executive Officer may, by notice in writing, require the owner, lessee or occupier of such building or land to clean, lime-wash internally or externally, clear, or otherwise put such building or land in a proper state within such period as may be specified in the notice.	(2) Any person who fails to comply with the notice issued under sub-section (1) shall be 1[liable to penalty which may extend to five thousand rupees and, in the case of continuing default, to an additional penalty which may extend to two hundred and fifty rupees for each day after the first during which the default continues].
