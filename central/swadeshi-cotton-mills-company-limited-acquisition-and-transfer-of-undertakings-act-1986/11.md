# The Swadeshi Cotton Mills Company Limited (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1986, section 11
  On the vesting of a textile undertaking in the National Textile Corporation, all persons in charge of the management of a textile undertaking immediately before such vesting shall be bound to deliver to the National Textile Corporation, all assets, books of account, registers or other documents in their custody relating to the textile undertaking.
