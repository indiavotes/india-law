# The Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, section 85A
  1[85A. Drafts drawn by one branch of a bank on another payable to order.--where any draft, that is an order to pay money, drawn by one office of a bank upon another office of the same bank for a sum of money payable to order on demand, purports to be endorsed by or on behalf of the payee, the bank is discharged by payment in due course.]
