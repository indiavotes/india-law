# The National Sports Governance Act, 2025, section 14
  (1) Only a recognised sports organisation shall be eligible to receive grants or any other financial assistance from the Central Government.
(2) A recognised sports organisation, receiving grants or any other financial assistance from the Central Government under sub-section (1) or from a State Government, shall be considered as a public authority under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (22 of 2005), with respect to utilisation of such grants or any other financial assistance.
