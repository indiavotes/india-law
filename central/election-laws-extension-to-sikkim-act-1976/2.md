# The Election Laws (Extension to Sikkim) Act, 1976, section 2
  (1) The Acts mentioned in the Schedule are hereby extended to, and shall be in force in, the State of Sikkim.
(2) With effect from the commencement of this Act, the Acts mentioned in the Schedule shall be amended as specified therein.
(3) Any reference in the Acts mentioned in the Schedule to a law not in force, or to a functionary not in existence, in the State of Sikkim shall be construed as a reference to the corresponding law in force, or to the corresponding functionary in existence, in that State:
Provided that if any question arises as to who such corresponding functionary is, or if there is no such corresponding functionary, the Central Government shall decide as to who such functionary will be and the decision of the Central Government shall be final.
