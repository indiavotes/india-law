# The National Company Limited (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1980, section 35
  (1) The National Company Limited (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Ordinance, 1980 (4 of 1980), is hereby repealed.
(2) Notwithstanding such repeal, anything done or any action taken under the Ordinance so repealed shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provisions of this Act.
