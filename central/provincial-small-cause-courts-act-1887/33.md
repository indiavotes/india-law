# The Provincial Small Cause Courts Act, 1887, section 33
  A Court invested with the jurisdiction of a Court of Small Causes with respect to the exercise of that jurisdiction, and the same Court with respect to the exercise of its jurisdiction in suits of a civil nature which are not cognizable by a Court of Small Causes, shall, for the purposes of this Act and the 1Code of Civil Procedure (14 of 1882), be deemed to be different Courts.
