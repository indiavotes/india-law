# The National Medical Commission Act, 2019, section 40
  Where the Commission deems it necessary, it may, by an order published in the Official Gazette, direct that any medical qualification granted by a medical institution in a country outside India, after such date as may be specified in that notification, shall be a recognised medical qualification for the purposes of this Act:
Provided that medical practice by a person possessing such qualification shall be permitted only if such person qualifies National Exit Test.
