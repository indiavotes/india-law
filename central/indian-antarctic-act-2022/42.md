# The Indian Antarctic Act, 2022, section 42
  Where the contravention involves a vessel under this Act, the Operator of such vessel shall be punishable,---
(a) for contravention of section 6 or section 11 or section 12 or section 13 or section 18 or section 19 or section 21 or section 22, with imprisonment for a term which may extend to three years, or with fine which shall not be less than one crore rupees but which may extend to five crore rupees,or with both;
(b) for contravention of section 7 or section 9 or section 39, with imprisonment for a term which may extend to seven years and with fine which shall not be less than two crore rupees but which may extend to ten crore 1rupees.
