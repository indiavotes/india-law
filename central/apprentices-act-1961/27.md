# The Apprentices Act, 1961, section 27
  (1) The Government 1[may appoint suitable persons as Additional, Joint, Regional, Deputy and Assistant Apprenticeship Advisers] to assist the Apprenticeship Adviser in the performance of his functions.
(2) 2[Every Additional, Joint, Regional, Deputy or Assistant Apprenticeship Adviser shall, subject to the control of the Apprenticeship Adviser], perform such functions as may be assigned to him by the Apprenticeship Adviser.
