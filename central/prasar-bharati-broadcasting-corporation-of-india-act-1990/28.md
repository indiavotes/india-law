# The Prasar Bharati (Broadcasting Corporation of India) Act, 1990, section 28
  No suit or other legal proceeding shall lie against the Corporation,the Chairman or any Member or officer or other employee thereof or the President or a member of the Broadcasting Council or a member of a Regional Council or a Recruitment Board for anything which is in good faith done or intended to be done in pursuance of this Act or of any rules on regulations made thereunder.
