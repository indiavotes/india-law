# The Unified Waqf Management, Empowerment, Efficiency and Development Act, 1995, section 18
  (1) The Board may, whenever it considers necessary, establish either generally or for a particular purpose or for any specified area or areas committees for the supervision of 1[auqaf].
(2) The constitution, functions and duties and the term of office of such committees shall be determined from time to time by the Board:
Provided that it shall not be necessary for the members of such committees to be members of the Board.
