# The High Court Judges Salaries and Conditions of Service Act, 1954, section 13A
  1 2[13A. Salaries of the Judges.(1) There shall be paid to the Chief Justice of a High Court, by way of salary, 3[ 4[two lakh fifty thousand rupees per mensem] ].
(2) There shall be paid to a Judge of a High Court, by way of salary, 5[ 6[two lakh twenty-five thousand rupees per mensem].]
