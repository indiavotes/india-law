# The Prevention of Money-Laundering Act, 2002, section 15
1[15. Procedure and manner of furnishing information by reporting entities.-- The Central Government may, in consultation with the Reserve Bank of India, prescribe the procedure and the manner of maintaining and furnishing information by a reporting entity under 2[section 11A, sub-section (1) of section 12 and sub-section (1) of section 12AA] for the purpose of implementing the provisions of this Act.]
