# The Weapons of Mass Destruction and their Delivery Systems (Prohibition of Unlawful Activities) Act, 2005, section 9
  No person shall, directly or indirectly, transfer to a non-State actor or terrorist, any material, equipment and technology notified under this Act or any other Act related to relevant activity:
Provided that such transfer made to a non-State actor shall not include a transfer made as such to any person acting under lawful authority in India.
