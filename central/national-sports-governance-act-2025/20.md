# The National Sports Governance Act, 2025, section 20
  The Tribunal shall not have jurisdiction to adjudicate the following matters, namely:---
(a) any dispute, or conflict arising during the Olympic Games, Paralympic Games, Commonwealth Games, Asian Games, or any other similar events organised by any International Federations;
(b) any dispute falling within the exclusive jurisdiction of---
(i) the internal disputes resolution committee of a National Sports Body; or
(ii) any other tribunal or court; or
(iii) any International Federation; or
(iv) the Court of Arbitration for Sports established in Lausanne, Switzerland;
(c) doping related disputes, where Disciplinary Panels constituted under the National Anti-Doping Act, 2022 (15 of 2022) have exclusive jurisdiction;
(d) any matter in respect of which the International Charters and Statutes require the parties to submit to specific mechanism or forum for adjudication.
