# The Oilfields (Regulation and Development) Act, 1948, section 1
  (1) This Act may be called The1[Oilfields] (Regulation and Development) Act, 1948.
2[(2) It extends to the whole of India3***].
(3) it shall come into force on such date4 as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint in this behalf.
