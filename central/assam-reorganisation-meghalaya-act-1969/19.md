# The Assam Reorganisation (Meghalaya) Act, 1969, section 19
  (1) The Governor shall, from time to time summon the Legislative Assembly to meet at such time and place as he thinks fit, but six months shall not intervene between its last sitting in one session and the date appointed for its first sitting in the next session.
(2) The Governor may, from time to time,—
(a) prorogue the Legislative Assembly;
(b) dissolve the Legislative Assembly.
