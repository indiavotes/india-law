# The Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, section 7B
  1[7B. Annual report of activities financed under the Fund.--The Central Government shall, as soon as may be, after the end of each financial year, cause to be published in the Official Gazette, a report giving an account of the activities financed under section 7A during the financial year, together with a statement of accounts.]
