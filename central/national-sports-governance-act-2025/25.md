# The National Sports Governance Act, 2025, section 25
  (1) Unless the International Charters and Statutes provide for appeal before the Court of Arbitration for Sport, an appeal shall lie against any order of the Tribunal, not being an interlocutory order, to the Supreme Court.
(2) No appeal shall lie against any decision made by the Tribunal with the consent of the parties.
(3) Every appeal under this section shall be preferred within a period of thirty days from the date of the decision appealed against:
Provided that the Supreme Court may entertain the appeal after the expiry of the said period of thirty days, if it is satisfied that the appellant was prevented by sufficient cause from preferring the appeal in time.
