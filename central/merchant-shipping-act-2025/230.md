# The Merchant Shipping Act, 2025, section 230
  (1) The Central Government may make rules to carry out the provisions of this Part.
(2) In particular, and without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing power, such rules may provide for all or any of the following matters, namely:---
(a) the circumstances under which the nodal authority shall carry out maritime emergency response to a marine incident under sub-section (4) of section 228;
(b) adequate insurance coverage or other financial security under section 229;
(c) any other matter which is required to be or may be prescribed.
