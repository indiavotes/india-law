# The Delhi Rent Control Act, 1958, section 14D
  1[14D. Right to recover immediate possession of premises to accrue to a widow.-- (1) Where the landlord is a widow and the premises let out by her, or by her husband, are required by her for her own residence, she may apply to the Controller for recovering the immediate possession of such premises.
(2) Where the landlord referred to in sub-section (1) has let out more than one premises, it shall he open to her to make an application under that sub-section in respect of any one of the premises chosen by her.]
