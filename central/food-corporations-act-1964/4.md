# The Food Corporations Act, 1964, section 4
  (1) The head office of the Corporation shall be at Madras or at such other place as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, specify.
[1 (2) The Corporation may establish offices or agencies at other places in or outside India:
Provided that no such office or agency shall be established at any place outside India without the previous approval of the Central Government.]
