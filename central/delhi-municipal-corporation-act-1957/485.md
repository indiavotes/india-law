# The Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 1957, section 485
  485. Power of Central Government 1 [or the Government] to require production of documents.---The Central Government 1 [or the Government] may at any time require the Commissioner--
(a) to produce any record, correspondence, plan or other document in his possession or under his control;
(b) to furnish any return, plan, estimate, statement, account or statistics relating to the proceedings, duties or works of 2[3[the Corporation]] or any of the municipal authorities;
(c) to furnish or obtain and furnish any report.
