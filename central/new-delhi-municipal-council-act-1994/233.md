# The New Delhi Municipal Council Act, 1994, section 233
  The Chairperson shall--
(a) take measures for lighting in a suitable manner all such public streets and public places as may be specified by him;
(b) procure, erect and maintain such number of lamps, lamp posts and other appurtenances as may be necessary for the said purpose;
(c) cause such lamps to be lighted by means of oil, electricity or such other light as he may determine.
