# The Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, section 210C
1[210C. Power of Central Government to make rules.--The Central Government may make rules for--(a) design, construction and maintenance standards for National highways;(b) such other factors as may be taken into account by the Court under sub-section (3) of section 198A;2[(ba) the manner in which warning for contraventions shall be recorded under this Chapter;](c) any other matter which is, or has to be, prescribed by the Central Government.]
