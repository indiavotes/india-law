# The Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 2016., section 51
  The Authority may, by general or special order in writing, delegate to any 1[Member or officer] of the Authority or any other person, subject to such conditions, if any, as may be specified in the order, such of its powers and functions under this Act (except the power under section 54) as it may deem necessary.
