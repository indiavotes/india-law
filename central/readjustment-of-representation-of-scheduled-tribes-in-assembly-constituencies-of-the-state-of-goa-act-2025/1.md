# The Readjustment of Representation of Scheduled Tribes in Assembly Constituencies of the State of Goa Act, 2025, section 1
  (1) This Act may be called the Readjustment of Representation of Scheduled Tribes in Assembly Constituencies of the State of Goa Act, 2025.
(2) It shall come into force on such date1 as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.
