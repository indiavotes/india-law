# The Protection of Plant Varieties and Farmers Rights Act, 2001, section 65
  (1) No suit--
(a) for the infringement of a variety registered under this Act; or
(b) relating to any right in a variety registered under this Act,
shall be instituted in any court inferior to a District Court having jurisdiction to try the suit.
(2) For the purposes of clauses (a) and (b) of sub-section (1), District Court having jurisdiction shall mean the District Court within the local limit of whose jurisdiction the cause of action arises.
