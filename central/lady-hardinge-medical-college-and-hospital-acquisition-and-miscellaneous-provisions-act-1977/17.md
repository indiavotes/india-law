# The Lady Hardinge Medical College and Hospital (Acquisition and Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1977, section 17
  Notwithstanding anything contained in the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 (2 of 1974), no court shall take cognizance of any offence under this Act except on a complaint, in writing, made by the Central Government or any officer authorised in this behalf by that Government.
