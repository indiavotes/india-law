# The Illegal Migrants (Determination by Tribunals) Act, 1983, section 13
Every reference made to a Tribunal under 1[section 8 or section 8A or application made to a Tribunal under section 8] shall be inquired into as expeditiously as possible and every endeavour shall be made to conclude such inquiry within a period of six months from the date of the service, on the person concerned, of a copy of such reference or application.
