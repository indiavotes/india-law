# The Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, section 194D
1[194D. Penalty for not wearing protective headgear.-- Whoever drives a motor cycle or causes or allows a motor cycle to be driven in contravention of the provisions of section 129 or the rules or regulations made thereunder shall be 2[liable for a penalty] of one thousand rupees and he shall be disqualified for holding licence for a period of three months.]
