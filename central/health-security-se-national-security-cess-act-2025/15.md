# The Health Security Se National Security Cess Act, 2025, section 15
  (1) All officers of police, railways, customs and those officers engaged in the collection of land revenue, including village officers, officers of the State Governments and Union territory Administrations shall assist the proper officer in the implementation of this Act.
(2) The Central Government may, by notification, empower and require any other class of officers to assist the proper officers in the implementation of this Act when called upon to do so.
