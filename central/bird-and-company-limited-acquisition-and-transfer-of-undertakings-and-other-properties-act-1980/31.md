# The Bird and Company Limited (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings and Other Properties) Act, 1980, section 31
  (1) The Bird and Company Limited (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings and Other Properties) Ordinance, 1980 (18 of 1980), is hereby repealed.
(2) Notwithstanding such repeal, anything done or any action taken under the Ordinance so repealed shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provisions of this Act.
