# The Coastal Shipping Act, 2025, section 18
If any licensee or agent on whom a notice has been served under sub-section (2) of section 35---         (a) fails to furnish the information required within the time specified; or        (b) furnishes any information or makes any statement which the licensee or agent knows to be false on any material particular,he shall be 1[liable to penalty which may extend to two lakh rupees], and the vessel shall also be liable for detention under section 29.
