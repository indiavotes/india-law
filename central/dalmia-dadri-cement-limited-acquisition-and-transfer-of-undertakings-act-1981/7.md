# The Dalmia Dadri Cement Limited (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1981, section 7
  For the transfer to, and vesting in, the Central Government, under section 3, of the undertakings of the Company and the right, title and interest of the Company in relation to its undertakings, there shall be paid by the central Government to the Company, in cash, and in the manner specified in Chapter VI, an amount of rupees eighty-four lakhs and eighty-seven thousand.
