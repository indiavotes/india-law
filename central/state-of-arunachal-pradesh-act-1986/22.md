# The State of Arunachal Pradesh Act, 1986, section 22
  The law in force immediately before the appointed day with respect to the form of writs and other processes used, issued or awarded by the High Court of Assam, Nagaland, Meghalaya, Manipur, Tripura and Mizoram shall, with necessary modifications, apply with respect to the form of writs and other processes used, issued or awarded by the common High Court.
