# The Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 1957, section 116I
  1[116I. Validity of base unit area values of vacant land and covered space of building.---The base unit area value of vacant land and the base unit area value of covered space of building in respect of a specified group in a ward shall remain in force for a period of three years:
Provided that till the revision of such base unit area values is completed, the existing base unit area values shall continue to be in force.
