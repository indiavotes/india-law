# The Insurance Act, 1938, section 64C
  1[64C. Councils of Life Insurance and General Insurance.--On and from the date of commencement of this Act, --
(a) the existing Life Insurance Council, a representative body of the insurers, who carry on the life insurance business in India; and
(b) the existing General Insurance Council, a representative body of insurers, who carry on general, health insurance business and re-insurance in India,
shall be deemed to have been constituted as the respective Councils under this Act.]
