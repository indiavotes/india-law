# The Requisitioning and Acquisition of Immovable Property Act, 1952, section 21
The competent authority, every arbitrator and every officer empowered by the Central Government or the competent authority, while exercising any power or performing any duty under this Act, shall be deemed to be a public servant within the meaning of 1[clause (28) of section 2 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (45 of 2023)].
