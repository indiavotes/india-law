# The Assam University Act, 1989, section 6
  1 [(1) The jurisdiction of the University shall extend to the Cachar, Karimganj, North Cachar Hills, Karbi Anglong and Hailakandi Districts of the State of Assam.]
(2) On and from the commencement of this Act, all Colleges situated in the districts of Cachar, Karimganj, North Cachar Hills 2 [, Karbi Anglong and Hailkandi] shall stand affiliated to the University.
