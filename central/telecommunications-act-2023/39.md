# The Telecommunications Act, 2023., section 39
  Any person aggrieved by an order of the Designated Appeals Committee under section 36, in so far as it pertains to matters under sub-section (1) of section 32, or an order of the Central Government under sub-section (2) of section 32, may prefer an appeal to the Telecom Disputes Settlement and Appellate Tribunal constituted under section 14 of the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India Act, 1997 (24 of 1997), within a period of thirty days from the date on which a copy of the order is received by such authorised entity or assignee.
