# The Geographical Indications of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act, 1999, section 43
  43. [Penalty for improperly describing a place of business as connected with the Geographical Indications Registry.]— Omitted by The Jan Vishwas (amendment of Provisions) Act, 2023 (18 of 2023), s. 2 and Schedule (w.e.f. 1-8-2024).
