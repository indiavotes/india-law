# The Customs Act, 1962, section 64
  1[64. Owner's right to deal with warehoused goods.--The owner of any warehoused goods may, after warehousing the same,--
(a) inspect the goods;
(b) deal with their containers in such manner as may be necessary to prevent loss or deterioration or damage to the goods;
(c) sort the goods; or
(d) show the goods for sale.]
