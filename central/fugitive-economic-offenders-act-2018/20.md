# The Fugitive Economic Offenders Act, 2018, section 20
  (1) The Central Government may, having regard to the objects of this Act, and if it considers necessary or expedient so to do, by notification add to, or as the case may be, omit from the Schedule any offences specified therein.
(2) Every such notification shall, as soon as after it is issued, be laid before each House of Parliament.
