# The Recycling of Ships Act, 2019, section 15
  (1) Every Ship Recycler shall provide adequate measures for safety, health, training and welfare of workers in his ship recycling facility and for this purpose, the provisions of the Factories Act, 1948 (63 of 1948) shall apply.
(2) Every Ship Recycler shall provide an individual or comprehensive insurance coverage for the regular and temporary workers in such manner as may be prescribed.
