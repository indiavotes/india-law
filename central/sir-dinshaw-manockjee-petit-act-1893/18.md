# The Sir Dinshaw Manockjee Petit Act, 1893, section 18
  It shall be lawful for the Corporation out of the money which shall come to their hands by virtue of the trusts and provisions of this Act to retain and reimburse themselves all costs, damages and expenses which they shall or may sustain, expend or disburse in or about the execution of the aforesaid powers; trusts and provisions, or in relation thereto.
