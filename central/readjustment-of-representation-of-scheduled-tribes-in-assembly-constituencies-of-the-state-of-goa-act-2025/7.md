# The Readjustment of Representation of Scheduled Tribes in Assembly Constituencies of the State of Goa Act, 2025, section 7
  (1) The Commission may, from time to time, by notification in the Gazette of India and in the Official Gazette of the State,---
(a) correct any printing mistake in the Delimitation Order as amended under this Act, or any error occurring therein from any inadvertent slip or omission; and
(b) where the boundaries or the name of any district or any territorial division mentioned in the said Order are or is altered, make such amendments as appear to it to be necessary or expedient for bringing the Order up-to-date.
(2) Every notification under this section shall be laid, as soon as may be after it is issued, before the House of the People and the Legislative Assembly of the State.
