# The Industries (Development and Regulation) Act, 1951, section 28
  1[28. Burden of proof in certain cases.--Where any person is 1 1[imposed penalty] for contravening any order made under section 18G which prohibits him from doing an act or being in possession of a thing without lawful authority or without a permit, licence or other document, the burden of proving that he has such authority, permit, licence or other document shall be on him.
