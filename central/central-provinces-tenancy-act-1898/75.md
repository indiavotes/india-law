# The Central Provinces Tenancy Act, 1898, section 75
  (1) In suits between landlords and tenants as such, the plaint shall, in addition to the matters mentioned in section 50 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1882 (XIV of 1882), specify the area of the land to which the suit relates, and, where the fields comprised in that land have been numbered in a Government survey, the number of each such field ; and, in the case of suits for an arrear, the amount of the yearly rent and the instalments in which it is payable.
(2) When the land to which the suit relates comprises parts of numbered fields or has not been divided into numbered fields, an accurate and sufficient description of the land and its boundaries shall be given in the plaint.
