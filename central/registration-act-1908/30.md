# The Registration Act, 1908, section 30
  (1) Any Registrar may in his discretion receive and register any document which might be registered by any Sub-Registrar subordinate to him.

STATE AMENDMENT
Karnataka
Amendment of section 30.--In Section 30 of the Registration Act, 1908 (Central Amendment 16 of 1908) (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act), the brackets and figure (1) and 'sub-section (2)' shall be omitted.]
[Vide Karnataka Act 24 of 1989, s. 2]
Orissa
Amendment of section 30.--In the Registration Act, 1908 (16 of 1908) (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act), in section 30, sub-section (2) shall be omitted.
[Vide Orissa Act 19 of 1991, s. 2]
Haryana
Amendment of section 30 of Central Act 16 of 1908.--Sub-section (2) of section 30 of the Registration Act, 1908 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act), shall be omitted.
[Vide Haryana Act 4 of 1997, s. 2]
