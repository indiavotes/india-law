# The National Trust for Welfare of Persons with Autism, Cerebral Palsy, Mental Retardation and Multiple Disabilities Act, 1999, section 5
  (1) The Chairperson may resign his office by writing under his hand addressed to the Central Government:
Provided that the Chairperson shall continue in office 1[until the appointment of his successor is made by the Central Government].
(2) A Member may resign from office by writing under his hand addressed to the Chairperson.
