# The Registration Act, 1908, section 28
  Save as in this Part otherwise provided, every document mentioned in section 17, sub-section (1), clauses (a), (b), (c) 1 [, (d) and (e), section 17, sub-section (2), insofar as such document affects immovable property,]and section 18, clauses (a), (b) 2[(c) and (cc),] shall be presented for registration in the office of a Sub-Registrar within whose sub-district the whole or some portion of the property to which such document relates is situate.

(i) for the word, brackets and letter and (e) , the commas, brackets, letters and word, (e), (f) and (g) shall be substituted; and
