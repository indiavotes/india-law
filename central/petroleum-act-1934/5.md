# The Petroleum Act, 1934, section 5
  (1) No one shall produce, refine or blend petroleum save in accordance with the rules made under sub-section (2).
(2) The 1[Central Government] may makes rules--
(a) prescribing the conditions subject to which petroleum may be produced, refined or blended; and
(b) regulating the removal of petroleum from places where it is produced, refined or blended and preventing the storage therein and removal therefrom, except as 2[petroleum Class A], of any petroleum, which has not satisfied the prescribed tests.
