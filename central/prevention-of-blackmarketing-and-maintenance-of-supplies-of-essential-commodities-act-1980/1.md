# The Prevention of Blackmarketing and Maintenance of Supplies of Essential Commodities Act, 1980, section 1
    (1) This Act may be called the Prevention of Black marketing and Maintenance of Supplies of Essential Commodities Act, 1980.
(2) It extends to the whole of India 1***.
(3) It shall be deemed to have come into force on the 15th day of October, 1979.
