# The National Institutes of Pharmaceutical Education and Research Act, 1998, section 37
  (1) The National Institute of Pharmaceutical Education and Research Ordinance, 1998 (Ord. 9 of 1998) is hereby repealed.
(2) Notwithstanding such repeal, anything done or any action taken under the said Ordinance, shall be deemed to have been done or taken under this Act.
