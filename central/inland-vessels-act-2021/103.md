# The Inland Vessels Act, 2021, section 103
  Whoever contravenes any of the provisions of this Act or the rules made thereunder, shall be triable for the offence in any place where he may be found or at the place of occurrence or at the place in the State where the offence has been committed or a place which the Central Government or the State Government, as the case may be, by notification, specify in this behalf, or any other place in which he might be tried under any other enactment for the time being in force.
