# The Road Transport Corporations Act, 1950, section 46
The State Government may, by rule, provide that the breach of any rules made by it under section 44 1[or any regulations made by a Corporation under section 45] 2[shall be liable to penalty which may extend to five hundred rupees and when the breach is a continuing one with a further penalty not exceeding twenty rupees for every day after the first contravention, during which the breach continues].
