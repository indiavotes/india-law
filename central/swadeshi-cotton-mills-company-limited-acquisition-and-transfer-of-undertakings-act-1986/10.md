# The Swadeshi Cotton Mills Company Limited (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1986, section 10
  The National Textile Corporation or any person which that Corporation may, by order in writing, specify, shall be entitled to exercise the powers of general superintendence, direction, control and management of the affairs and business of a textile undertaking, the right, title and interest of the Company in relation to which have vested in that Corporation under sub-section (2) of section 3, and do all such things as the Company is authorised to exercise and do.
