# The Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisement) Act, 1954, section 1
  (1) This Act may be called the Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisement) Act, 1954.
(2) It extends to the whole of India 1***, and applies also to persons domiciled in the territories to which this Act extends who are outside the said territories.
(3) It shall come into force on such date2 as the Central Government may, by Notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.
