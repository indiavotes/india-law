# The Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, section 87
The Members, officers of the Food Authority and the Commissioners of Food Safety and their officers shall be deemed, when acting or purporting to act in pursuance of any of the provisions of this Act, to be public servants within the meaning of 1[clause (28) of section 2 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (45 of 2023)].
