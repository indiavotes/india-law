# The Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016., section 11
  (1) No individual shall, without the authorisation of the Bureau, in any manner or form, publish, reproduce or record any Indian Standard or part thereof, or any other publication of the Bureau.
(2) No person shall issue a document that creates, or may create the impression that it is or contains an Indian Standard, as contemplated in this Act:
Provided that nothing in this sub-section shall prevent any individual from making a copy of Indian Standard for his personal use.
