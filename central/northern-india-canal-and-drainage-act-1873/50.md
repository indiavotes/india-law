# The Northern India Canal and Drainage Act, 1873, section 50
  Any fine imposed under this Act upon the owner of any vessel, or the servant or agent of such owner or other person in charge of any vessel, for any offence in respect of the navigation of such vessel, may be recovered either in the manner prescribed by the Code of Criminal Procedure 1 or, if the Magistrate imposing the fine so directs, as though it were a charge due in respect of such vessel.
