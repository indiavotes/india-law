# The National Institutes of Pharmaceutical Education and Research Act, 1998, section 14
  Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Statutes and the Ordinances, the 1[senate of each Institute] shall have the control and general regulation, and be responsible for the maintenance of standards of instruction, education and examination in the Institute and shall exercise such other powers and perform such other duties as may be conferred or imposed upon it by the Statutes.
