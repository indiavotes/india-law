# The Trade Marks Act, 1999, section 10
  (1) A trade mark may be limited wholly or in part to any combination of colours and any such limitation shall be taken into consideration by the 1[Registrae or the High Court, as the case may be,] having to decide on the distinctive character of the trade mark.
(2) So far as a trade mark is registered without limitation of colour, it shall be deemed to be registered for all colours.
