# The International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019, section 1
  (1) This Act may be called the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019.
(2) It shall come into force on such date1 as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint; and different dates may be appointed for different provisions of this Act, and any reference in any provision to the commencement of this Act shall be construed as a reference to the coming into force of that provision.
