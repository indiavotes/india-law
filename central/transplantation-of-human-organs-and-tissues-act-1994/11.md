# The Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Act, 1994, section 11
  11. Prohibition of removal or transplantation of 2 [human organs or tissues or both] for any purpose other than the rapeatic purposes.-- No donor and no person empowered to give authority for the removal of any human organ shall authorise the removal of any 1[human organ or tissue or both] for any purpose other than therapeutic purposes.
