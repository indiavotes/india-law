# The Andhra Pradesh and Mysore (Transfer of Territory) Act, 1968, section 7
  (1) Any reference in the Delimitation of Council Constituencies (Andhra Pradesh) Order, 1957, to Anantapur district shall be construed as including the territory transferred to that district from the State of Mysore.
(2) Any reference in the Delimitation of Council Constituencies (Mysore) Order, 1951, to Kolar district shall be construed as excluding the territory transferred from that district to the State of Andhra Pradesh.
