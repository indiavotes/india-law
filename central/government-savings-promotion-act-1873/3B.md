# The Government Savings Promotion Act, 1873, section 3B
  1[3B. Deposit by minor.--(1) A minor who has attained the age of ten years may open and operate an account in the Government Savings Bank, if so permitted under a Savings Scheme.
(2) Subject to the provisions of sub-section (1), the guardian of a minor may open and operate an account on behalf of the minor, till he becomes a major.]
