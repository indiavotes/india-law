# The Cantonments Act, 2006, section 197
Whoever---		(a) uses for other than domestic purposes any water supplied by a Board for domestic use; or		(b) where water is supplied by agreement with a Board for a specified purpose, uses that water for any other purposes shall be 1[liable to penalty] which may extend to two thousand five hundred rupees, and in addition, the Board shall be entitled to recover from him the cost of the water misused.
