# The Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014, section 98
  In section 15A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, after the words and figures "under the Tamil Nadu Legislative Council Act, 2010 (16 of 2010)", the words and figures "and constituting the Legislative Council of the State of Telangana under the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014" shall be inserted.
