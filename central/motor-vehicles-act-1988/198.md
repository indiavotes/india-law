# The Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, section 198
1[198. Unauthorised interference with vehicle.--- Whoever otherwise than with lawful authority or reasonable excuse, without prejudice to any liability under the provisions of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (45 of 2023),---		(a) enters or mounts any stationary motor vehicle shall be liable to penalty of one thousand rupees;		(b) tampers with the brake or any part of the mechanism of a motor vehicle shall be punishable with a fine of ten thousand rupees.]
